Connect your Udemy account

One-time setup. After this, connecting an account is a single click - no typing, and you handle any captcha or OTP in your own browser.
  1. 1
    Drag this button to your bookmarks bar
    If the bookmarks bar is hidden, press Ctrl+Shift+B first.
    📚 Send to Enroller
    Can't drag it? Right-click your bookmarks bar → Add page, and paste the link from "Copy link" below.
  2. 2
    Open udemy.com and log in
    Use the account you want to auto-enroll. Finish any captcha, OTP or 2-step check as normal.
  3. 3
    On the Udemy tab, click the "Send to Enroller" bookmark
    A new tab confirms the account is connected. Repeat for each Udemy account you want to add.

Security

The bookmark carries a private connect code tied to your account. If you ever shared it or want to invalidate the old bookmark, reset it here - then re-drag the button above.
