Connect your Udemy account

Paste your Udemy access_token. No install, nothing to solve - you stay logged in on Udemy as normal.
How to copy your access_token ▾
  1. Open udemy.com and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click → Inspect) to open developer tools.
  3. Go to the Application tab → left side Cookies → https://www.udemy.com.
  4. Click the access_token row, double-click its Value, and copy it (Ctrl+C).
  5. Paste it below and press Connect.
