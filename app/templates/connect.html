Connect your Udemy account

A one-time setup with our browser extension. After that, connecting any Udemy account is a single click - you handle login (and any code/checks) in your own browser, exactly like normal.
  1. 1
    Install the extension
    Download it, unzip, then in Chrome/Edge open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and click Load unpacked on the unzipped folder. (Firefox/Kiwi on Android also work.)
    ⇩ Download extension
  2. 2
    Pair it with this site
    Copy this pairing code and paste it into the extension once. It ties the extension to your account here.
    Site: {{ origin_host }}
  3. 3
    Log in to udemy.com, then click the extension
    Use the account you want to auto-enroll. Click the extension's Connect button - done. Repeat for each account.

Security

The pairing code is a private token tied to your account. Your Udemy password is never seen by this site - only the session the extension reads from your logged-in browser. If you ever shared the code, reset it here and re-pair the extension.
